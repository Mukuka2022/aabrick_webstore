It turns a room into a number of boxes. Measure, pick your tile, and it does the rest, including the part people get wrong, which is that you cannot buy part of a box.

1. Give it the area

Two ways, and the buttons at the top choose between them. Length and width is for when you have a tape measure: type the metres and it multiplies them. I know the square metres is for when a builder has already given you a figure, so you are not measuring a room twice.

Tiling more than one room? Add another area and it adds them together. An L shaped room is two rectangles, so give it two.

2. Pick the tile

Choose one from the list and the size, the pieces per box and the price fill themselves in from our catalogue, so the answer is priced at what we actually charge. If the tile is not on the list, or you are pricing something you already have, choose Enter my own measurements and type the size, how many are in a box and what a box costs.

3. Leave the wastage alone unless you know better

It starts at 10 per cent, which is the normal figure rather than a generous one. Every tile that meets a wall is cut and the offcut is usually useless.

  • 5%A plain rectangle with few cuts, laid straight
  • 10%Most rooms. Leave it here if unsure
  • 15%Diagonal laying, large tiles, or lots of corners and pipes

What it tells you

The big number is the boxes to buy. Under it, the sums it used: the area, the area with wastage added, what one box covers, the tile count and the estimated cost.

A worked example. A room 5m by 4m is 20 square metres. Add 10 per cent and that is 22. A box covering 1.44 square metres goes into 22 fifteen and a bit times, so it rounds up to 16 boxes. The cost is 16 boxes at the box price, not 22 square metres at a rate, because 16 boxes is what the counter will ring up.

Then put it straight in the basket

If you picked a tile from the list, Add boxes to cart puts that exact number in your basket. No retyping and no arithmetic at the till. You can also send the figures to us with Ask for a quotation if you would rather have it priced by a person.

The mistake worth avoiding

Order the whole job at once. Tile shade varies between batches, and buying half now and half next month is how a floor ends up in two colours. If we have to bring your tile in from the manufacturer that is 7 days, so work this out before the tiler is booked, not after.
