Measure the floor, add something for cuts, divide by what a box covers, and round up. That is the whole method. The judgement is in the "something".

Measure the area

Length times width, in metres. A room 5m by 4m is 20 square metres. If the room is an L, split it into two rectangles and add them together rather than trying to be clever with the shape.

Leave the fitted units out of it only if they are staying. If the kitchen cupboards might move one day, tile under them.

Add for cuts and breakages

Every tile that meets a wall gets cut, and the offcut is usually too small to use elsewhere. Some tiles break in the cutting and one or two arrive chipped. That is what the wastage figure covers.

  • 10%A square or rectangular room, laid straight
  • 15%Diagonal laying, or many corners, alcoves and pipes
  • 15%Large format tiles, where every cut wastes more

Ten per cent is not generous, it is the normal figure. If it turns out you did not need it, the spare tiles are the point of the next section.

Then divide by the box

Boxes cover about 1.44 square metres in most of our ranges. So 20 square metres plus 10 per cent is 22, and 22 divided by 1.44 is 15.3, which means sixteen boxes. You cannot buy part of a box.

The tile calculator does this for you and fills in the box size and the price once you pick a tile, and it will take several rooms at once. If a builder has already given you a figure in square metres you can type that in directly instead of measuring again.

Keep the spare

When the job is done, put the leftover tiles somewhere dry and remember where. A cracked tile in three years is a twenty minute repair if you have a match, and a re-tiled room if you do not. Batches change, and the same code bought later may not be the same shade.

Order the whole job in one go

For the same reason: one order, one batch, one shade. Do not buy half now and half at the end of the month.

If we have to bring your tile in, that is 7 days from the manufacturer. Order before the tiler is booked, not after, and check with your branch what is on the shelf today.

Do not forget the rest of it

  • AdhesiveUltra Tile Fix 2, 20kg bags, cement based
  • GroutColour matched to the tile or deliberately contrasting
  • Spacers and trimAsk your branch what suits the tile

Coverage per bag depends on the tile, the notch on the trowel and how flat the floor is, so ask at the counter with your square metres rather than guessing from a number on the internet.
